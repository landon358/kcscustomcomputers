Warranty & protection plans

Every machine leaves the bench with a 40-day workmanship warranty. A protection plan is the optional part — ongoing care for as long as you keep it.

Included with every build

40-day limited workmanship warranty

The warranty covers the work: components seated and secured properly, cables connected and routed properly, cooling mounted properly, BIOS and Windows set up properly. If something KC did caused the problem, KC fixes it and you pay nothing for the diagnosis or the labor.

It is not a parts warranty. A component that fails on its own is covered by whoever made it — and KC will help you make that claim.

  • 40 daysFrom the day you pick the machine up or it arrives.
  • $0 laborOn a confirmed workmanship defect, diagnosis included.
  • Carry-inService happens at the shop in Walled Lake. Shipping and pickup are not covered.
  • Email firstA claim counts as on time when you email before day 40 — even if the machine comes in later.
  • 3 daysTo report a scratch or a dent that was there on arrival, with photos.
  • $50Diagnostic fee if the problem turns out not to be covered. Waived on Premium.

Opening the case or adding a part does not void the warranty. Coverage can be denied for a problem that a modification actually caused — not for the modification itself. The full warranty is below.

Protection plans

Optional · cancel any time

A protection plan is a subscription to service, not longer warranty coverage. It does not replace the 40-day warranty and it does not pay for parts. What it covers is the care around the machine: getting seen first, diagnosis without a fee, cleaning, and KC's labor when a manufacturer approves a replacement.

Plans run month to month, renew on their own, and end no later than 36 months from purchase. Cancel and coverage runs to the end of the month you have paid for.
