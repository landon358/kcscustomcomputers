1. Limited Warranty

1.1. KCS Computers LLC (“KCS”) warrants that the computer system identified on the Customer’s invoice (“System”) will be assembled, installed, and initially configured using reasonable care and generally accepted computer-building practices.

1.2. This is a Limited Workmanship Warranty. It covers defects directly caused by KCS’s assembly, component installation, connections, or initial configuration. It is not a general guarantee against every hardware, software, compatibility, or performance problem.

1.3. This warranty applies only to the original purchaser and is nontransferable, except where applicable law provides otherwise.

2. Warranty Period

1.1. This warranty begins on the date the Customer picks up or receives the System and continues for forty consecutive calendar days.

1.2. A claim is timely when the Customer emails KCS before the 40-day period expires and provides enough information to identify the Customer, the System, and the reported problem. After KCS requests an in-person inspection, the Customer must make the System available to KCS within fourteen calendar days unless KCS approves an extension in writing. A delay may affect coverage when it prevents KCS from determining the original cause of the reported condition.

1.3. The Customer may bring the System to KCS after the warranty period expires if the problem was reported by email before expiration and the System is brought in within a reasonable time after KCS requests inspection. Warranty service does not restart the full 40-day period. Workmanship performed during an approved warranty repair is covered for the longer of:

  1. the remainder of the original warranty period; or
  2. fifteen calendar days after the repaired System is returned to the Customer. The additional 15-day period applies only to the repair performed by KCS.

3. Covered Workmanship

3.1. Covered workmanship defects may include:

  1. improperly seated components;
  2. loose or incorrectly connected cables;
  3. improperly secured hardware;
  4. incorrect fan, pump, storage, or front-panel connections;
  5. improper installation of cooling hardware;
  6. cable routing that interferes with normal operation;
  7. BIOS, firmware, driver, or operating-system settings incorrectly configured by KCS;
  8. physical damage directly caused by KCS during assembly or service; and
  9. another comparable failure directly caused by KCS’s work.

3.2. KCS will determine whether a problem is caused by workmanship after reasonable inspection and diagnosis.

3.3. Unless KCS makes a specific written performance promise, this warranty means only that the System should power on, start normally, recognize the hardware installed by KCS, operate with reasonable stability, and substantially match the specifications listed on the invoice.

3.4. Coverage applies only to the System and components identified in KCS’s invoice, build record, serial-number record, or delivery documentation. KCS may deny a claim involving a substituted component that KCS did not supply or install, except to the extent an unrelated KCS Workmanship Defect remains covered.

4. Component Defects

4.1. A hardware component may fail even when it was properly installed. A failed processor, graphics card, motherboard, memory module, storage device, power supply, cooling device, fan, or other component is not automatically evidence of defective KCS workmanship.

4.2. Individual components may be covered by separate warranties issued by their manufacturers, distributors, or suppliers.

4.3. Manufacturer claims, supplier returns, replacement approval, replacement availability, shipping, and replacement-part condition are controlled by the applicable manufacturer or supplier terms.

4.4. Unless KCS agrees otherwise in writing, this warranty does not require KCS to purchase or advance the cost of a replacement for an independently defective component.

4.5. A separate KCS component-claim policy may apply when manufacturer or supplier assistance is requested.

5. Warranty Remedy

5.1. When KCS confirms a covered workmanship defect, KCS will provide a reasonable corrective remedy without charging the Customer for covered diagnostic or corrective labor.

5.2. KCS may:

  1. reconnect or adjust an affected part;
  2. correct the initial configuration;
  3. repeat the affected assembly work;
  4. repair the workmanship defect;
  5. reinstall an affected component;
  6. replace property directly damaged by KCS; or
  7. refund the amount paid for the specific KCS assembly or configuration service that cannot reasonably be corrected.

5.3. KCS may select the reasonable remedy based on the nature of the defect, the availability of parts, the cost of correction, and whether the remedy will restore the System to its warranted condition.

5.4. A warranty claim does not automatically entitle the Customer to a full refund, a new computer, an upgrade, a loaner computer, or compensation for inconvenience or time without the System.

5.5. The Customer must provide KCS with a reasonable opportunity to inspect and correct the problem before seeking reimbursement for repair work performed elsewhere, except where applicable law provides otherwise.

6. Making a Claim

6.1. Warranty claims must be submitted by email to the warranty-contact address published on the official KCS website.

6.2. The Customer should include:

  1. full name;
  2. telephone number;
  3. invoice or order number;
  4. System serial number, if available;
  5. date of pickup or delivery;
  6. a description of the problem;
  7. when the problem began;
  8. relevant error messages;
  9. photographs or video when helpful; and
  10. any recent hardware, software, BIOS, or configuration changes.

6.3. KCS may begin with remote troubleshooting and may require the complete System to be brought to KCS before deciding whether the claim is covered.

6.4. Submitting a claim does not automatically establish warranty coverage.

7. Diagnostic Fee

7.1. There is no standard diagnostic fee for a confirmed covered workmanship claim.

7.2. If KCS determines that the reported problem is not covered, the Customer may be charged a diagnostic fee of $50 USD.

7.3. A different diagnostic fee may apply only when:

  1. the different fee was clearly disclosed on the official KCS website before diagnosis;
  2. the fee was disclosed before KCS began diagnosis; and
  3. the Customer authorized the fee.

7.4. The diagnostic fee may apply when KCS cannot reproduce the problem or determines that the condition is caused by a component defect, software, customer modification, accident, external equipment, environmental condition, or another excluded cause.

7.5. KCS will not perform additional paid work without Customer authorization. All proposed charges will be disclosed in USD before the work is performed.

8. Carry-In Service

8.1. Warranty service is provided at KCS’s designated place of business.

8.2. The Customer is responsible for bringing the System to KCS and retrieving it after service.

8.3. KCS does not pay shipping, delivery, pickup, courier, travel, or transportation expenses under this warranty.

8.4. Damage caused during Customer transportation, handling, or inadequate packaging is not covered unless KCS directly caused the damage or applicable law provides otherwise.

9. Exclusions

9.1. This warranty does not cover a problem caused or materially contributed to by:

  1. accidental damage, impact, dropping, crushing, or improper handling;
  2. liquid exposure;
  3. broken glass, ports, connectors, case parts, or pins after delivery;
  4. lightning, power surges, unstable electrical service, improper outlets, or incorrect voltage;
  5. fire, flood, excessive heat, excessive humidity, condensation, smoke, corrosion, insects, or animals;
  6. blocked ventilation, excessive dust, grease, debris, or inadequate maintenance;
  7. incompatible hardware or accessories;
  8. software installed, removed, or changed after delivery;
  9. viruses, malware, ransomware, unauthorized software, or account problems;
  10. operating-system or application updates;
  11. internet, network, server, subscription, or online-service problems;
  12. theft, loss, abuse, misuse, or deliberate damage; or
  13. operation outside the applicable component manufacturer’s specifications.

9.1.5. Ordinary dust accumulation or a Customer Modification does not automatically cancel the entire warranty. Coverage may be denied only to the extent the condition caused or contributed to the reported problem.

10. Customer Modifications

10.1. Opening the System, installing a component, changing a setting, or using another repair provider does not automatically void all warranty coverage.

10.2. KCS may deny coverage for a condition caused or materially contributed to by:

  1. improper component installation;
  2. incompatible hardware;
  3. third-party repair;
  4. overclocking or undervolting;
  5. changed power, voltage, thermal, fan, or pump settings;
  6. improper BIOS or firmware changes;
  7. failed firmware updates;
  8. custom liquid cooling;
  9. altered internal wiring;
  10. physical damage occurring during modification; or
  11. failure to disclose relevant modifications during diagnosis. An unrelated KCS workmanship defect may remain covered.

11. Cosmetic Conditions

11.1. A cosmetic defect or discrepancy that existed when the Customer received the System must be reported by email within 3 calendar days after pickup or delivery. A cosmetic claim must include clear photographs sufficient to identify the reported condition. KCS may compare the claim with photographs and records created before or at delivery.

11.2. The Customer should provide clear photographs or video.

11.3. This warranty does not cover scratches, dents, impact damage, ordinary wear, fingerprints, dust, minor finish differences, normal lighting or color variations, or cosmetic damage occurring after delivery unless directly caused by KCS workmanship.

12. Software and Performance

12.1. Except for initial setup performed incorrectly by KCS, this warranty does not cover:

  1. customer-installed or customer-modified software;
  2. malware or viruses;
  3. application or game bugs;
  4. forgotten passwords or lost account access;
  5. subscription or license expiration;
  6. online-service interruptions;
  7. operating-system or application updates; or
  8. deleted or altered system files.

12.2. Unless expressly promised in writing, KCS does not guarantee:

  1. a particular frame rate;
  2. a particular benchmark result;
  3. a specific temperature or noise level;
  4. compatibility with every application, game, peripheral, or future update;
  5. a particular internet speed; or
  6. performance for a specialized purpose that was not documented in writing before purchase.

13. Data and Privacy

13.1. The Customer is responsible for maintaining a current backup of all important data before bringing the System to KCS.

13.2. Computer diagnosis and repair may involve a risk of data loss, corruption, alteration, or deletion. KCS does not guarantee that files, programs, accounts, settings, or other information will be preserved.

13.3. The Customer authorizes KCS to access system settings, diagnostic logs, software, user accounts, and files only to the extent reasonably necessary to diagnose, test, or repair the System.

13.4. KCS will use reasonable care to limit access to information relevant to the authorized service.

13.5. KCS will obtain Customer authorization before intentionally erasing a storage device or reinstalling the operating system, unless the Customer previously provided written authorization.

14. Service Timing

14.1. KCS will use reasonable efforts to complete warranty service.

14.2. Unless KCS makes a specific written commitment, appointment dates and completion dates are estimates and are not guaranteed.

14.3. Service time may be affected by diagnosis, workload, Customer response time, component availability, manufacturer or supplier processing, delivery delays, illness, emergencies, severe weather, or other events outside KCS’s reasonable control.

14.4. KCS is not required to provide loaner, rental, or substitute equipment.

15. Limitation of Damages

15.1. To the extent permitted by applicable law, KCS is not responsible under this warranty for indirect, incidental, special, punitive, or consequential damages. Examples may include:

  1. lost profits or revenue;
  2. business interruption;
  3. loss of business opportunity;
  4. loss of use;
  5. rental or substitute-equipment costs;
  6. lost work, school, gaming, or personal time;
  7. inconvenience;
  8. lost subscriptions or software licenses; and
  9. data loss resulting from the Customer’s failure to maintain a reasonable backup.

15.2. Nothing in this warranty excludes or limits liability for fraud, intentional misconduct, personal injury where liability cannot legally be limited, or another right or remedy that applicable law does not permit KCS to exclude.

15.3. Nothing prevents the Customer from pursuing a remedy available under applicable law if KCS’s warranty remedy fails of its essential purpose

16. Implied Warranties and Legal Rights

16.1. This Limited Warranty does not disclaim or eliminate an implied warranty or consumer right that cannot lawfully be disclaimed or eliminated.

16.2. This document does not independently impose a 40-day limitation on the duration of an implied warranty. Nothing in this warranty eliminates:

  1. a nonwaivable consumer right;
  2. a remedy required by applicable law;
  3. liability that cannot legally be excluded;
  4. an authorized written promise made by KCS; or
  5. rights provided by a component manufacturer, distributor, or supplier.

16.3. This warranty gives the Customer specific legal rights. The Customer may also have other rights that vary by jurisdiction.

17. Michigan Law and Venue

17.1. To the extent permitted by applicable law, this warranty is governed by the laws of the State of Michigan.

17.2. A legal proceeding relating to this warranty may be brought in a court with lawful jurisdiction located in Oakland County, Michigan.

17.3. This provision does not prevent the Customer from using another lawful venue, small-claims procedure, government complaint process, or remedy that cannot legally be waived.

17.4. This warranty does not independently require mandatory arbitration. A dispute may be subject to a separate arbitration agreement accepted by KCS and the Customer.

18. Separate Care Plan

18.1. The KCS Care Plan is an optional paid service agreement.

18.2. It is separate from this Limited Warranty and does not replace, reduce, or condition any right provided under this warranty.

18.3. Care Plan services, limits, exclusions, term, price, cancellation rights, and other conditions are governed by the separate Care Plan agreement.

19. Complete Warranty

19.1. This document, together with any authorized System-specific warranty term stated on the Customer’s invoice or written order documents, constitutes the complete KCS Limited Workmanship Warranty.

19.2. An oral statement does not modify this warranty unless KCS confirms the statement in an authorized writing.

19.3. If part of this warranty is unenforceable, that part will be limited only to the extent necessary, and the remaining provisions will continue in effect.

19.4. Applicable federal, state, and local law controls where it provides a right or requirement that cannot be waived.
